01 — About

Where craft meets
technical precision.

I'm a CG & Technical Artist with more than two years of professional experience building game-ready asset pipelines. With a foundation in Multimedia Technology from the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, I blend academic rigour with hands-on production knowledge to create tools and workflows that genuinely accelerate teams.

My focus sits at the intersection of art and engineering — whether that's writing procedural Houdini tools that save days of manual work, authoring HLSL shaders optimised for real-time engines, or building Maya Python utilities that let artists stay in their creative flow. I care deeply about scalable, well-documented pipelines that survive beyond the person who built them.
